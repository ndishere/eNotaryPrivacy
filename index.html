1. Who we are

eNotary Journal (“the app”) is a record-keeping tool for notaries public. It is published by Nawaldeep Singh (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at nawaldeepsingh2001@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

When you use the app, you may enter and save the following on your device:

  • Signer details: full name, address, phone number and email address
  • Identification details: ID type, ID number, issuing agency and expiration date
  • Notarial act details: act type, document title and date, fee charged and payment method
  • On-screen signatures, and a record of whether a physical thumbprint was taken on paper
  • Your notary commission profile: name, commission or license number, state and expiration date
  • PDF journal reports you export, and your app settings such as light or dark theme

This information stays in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to any server we operate, and we cannot see it.

3. Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no cloud sync. It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools that send information about you or your use of the app to us or to third parties.

4. Purchases

The app is free to start, with a one-time purchase to unlock unlimited entries. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play tells the app whether the purchase is valid so that your access can be unlocked or restored. Google’s handling of your payment information is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Backups, exports and sharing

  • Encrypted backups. When you create a backup, the app produces an encrypted file with the .enotary extension, protected with AES-256 encryption. You choose where it is saved, for example your phone storage or your own Google Drive. We never receive this file. Keep your backup safe, because we cannot recover it for you.
  • PDF reports. When you export a PDF journal, the file is created on your device. If you choose to share, email or print it, the app you share it with (for example an email app) handles the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Restoring. Restoring a backup reads the file you select. No data leaves your device.

Device backup settings. The app is set so that your journal data is not uploaded by Android’s automatic cloud backup. Backups happen only when you create them yourself.

6. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Files are saved and opened through Android’s system file picker, which means the app only reaches the files you choose.

7. Your responsibilities as a notary

The signer information you record can be sensitive, and you decide what to enter and how long to keep it. Because the data stays on your device, you are responsible for protecting it. We suggest that you:

  • Use a screen lock (PIN, pattern or biometric) on your phone
  • Keep your phone’s operating system up to date
  • Store backup files and their passwords securely
  • Follow your state’s laws on journal content, retention, security and disposal

Notary laws vary by state. eNotary Journal is a record-keeping tool and does not provide legal advice. Please confirm the requirements for your commission with your state’s notary authority.

8. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not have it. The only third party involved in using the app is Google Play, which handles app installation and purchases.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes the journal, signatures and reports saved inside the app. Backup files and PDF reports that you saved or shared outside the app are not removed automatically, so delete them yourself if you no longer want them. Check your state’s record retention rules before deleting any journal records.

10. Children

eNotary Journal is intended for adult professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete. You manage all of your data directly inside the app and on your device.
